About FraudShield-Pro Engine

Built for Bank Servers: This website exists primarily to demonstrate and deliver the FraudShield-Pro Engine—a modular, real-time fraud detection core designed so any bank server or payment gateway can plug it directly into their transaction pipeline to score, pause, or block fraudulent activity before settlement. The Live Payment Simulator is included solely as a testing harness to watch the engine work in real time.
Layer 1 Weighted Hybrid Risk Engine (ML + Behavioral + Velocity)

Every transaction is scored out of 100 across four weighted domains: 40% Machine Learning (30-tree Random Forest trained on historical fraud datasets + live resolved cases), 25% Behavioral Anomalies (cold-start-safe Z-score spending spikes, odd hours, high balance depletion), 15% Velocity & Structuring (1-minute bursts and micro-transaction sweeps), and 20% Device & Network Intelligence (VPNs, foreign geo, impossible travel).

Layer 2 Graph Intelligence & Money Mule Detection

Builds a live entity-relationship network connecting accounts, devices, and fund transfers. Automatically detects Shared-Device Fraud Rings, Money Mule Collectors (Fan-In), Rapid Distributors (Fan-Out), and Circular Transfer Loops using graph traversal.

Layer 3 Account Takeover (ATO) & Device Reputation

Tracks device trust profiles over time and detects suspicious session sequences—such as consecutive failed logins, unfamiliar devices, immediate high-value transfers right after login, or transfers immediately following a password change or new beneficiary addition. Severe ATO events trigger automatic, reversible 15-minute payment cooldowns.

Layer 4 ScamShield Pre-Payment Protection

Runs before a transfer is even submitted to catch authorized push-payment (APP) scams—warning customers about urgency or social-engineering keywords, newly added recipients, unusually large amounts, or abnormal recipient activity.

Layer 5 AI Investigator (Explainable AI) & Self-Learning Loop

Provides bank analysts with SHAP-style risk attribution bars, plain-English investigation briefs, counterfactual ("What-If") explanations, and downloadable SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) dossiers. Every analyst decision (Confirm Fraud or False Positive) feeds back into the engine's watchlist, device reputation, and chronological 80/20 benchmarked ML retraining pipeline.

How to Test FraudShield-Pro Engine

Step 1 Create Two Test Accounts (Sender & Recipient)

Open Payment Sandbox and register Account A (starts with ₹50,000). Then open an Incognito Window, visit the Payment Sandbox, and register Account B. Copy Account B's FSP-XXXXXXXX number.

Step 2 Test a Safe Transfer & ScamShield Warnings

In Account A, go to Send Money, paste Account B's number, enter ₹1,000 under Groceries / Food, and test a normal transfer. To trigger ScamShield, type urgency keywords like Urgent KYC refund or Police fine in the Note field before sending.

Step 3 Stress-Test Presets in the Transaction Simulator Tab

Inside the Payment Sandbox, click the Transaction Simulator tab. Use the one-click Safe Preset, Suspicious Preset, High-Risk Fraud, and Fraud Ring buttons (or enter custom amounts, hours, VPNs, and countries) and click Run Engine Evaluation to see instant verdicts.

Step 4 Test Account Takeover (ATO) Alerts

Sign out of Account A and enter the wrong password 3 times, then sign in with the right password. Open Security Center to view the logged suspicious login alert.

Step 5 Investigate Everything in the Bank Analyst SOC Portal

Open Bank Analyst SOC Portal and register an analyst account. Inspect live KPIs and charts, click nodes in the Fraud Network Graph (especially after running the Fraud Ring preset), review ATO & Device Intel, and click Investigate in the Audit Log to view risk breakdowns, resolve cases, and download SAR reports.
