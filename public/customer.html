    About FraudShield-Pro Engine

    Built for Bank Servers: This website exists primarily to demonstrate and deliver the FraudShield-Pro Engine—a modular, real-time fraud detection core designed so any bank server or payment gateway can plug it directly into their transaction pipeline to score, pause, or block fraudulent activity before settlement.
    Layer 1Weighted Hybrid Risk Engine

    Scores every transaction out of 100 across Machine Learning (40%), Behavioral Anomalies (25%), Velocity & Structuring (15%), and Device & Network Intelligence (20%).

    Layer 2Graph Intelligence & Money Mule Detection

    Detects Shared-Device Fraud Rings, Money Mule Collectors, Rapid Distributors, and Circular Transfer Loops.

    Layer 3ATO, Device Reputation & ScamShield

    Tracks device trust over time, catches suspicious login-to-transfer sequences, and warns customers against social-engineering scams before funds move.

    Layer 4AI Investigator (XAI) & Self-Learning Loop

    Provides SHAP-style risk attributions, counterfactuals, downloadable SAR dossiers, and one-click analyst feedback that retrains the model.

    How to Test FraudShield-Pro Engine

    Step 1Create Two Test Accounts

    Register Account A here (starts with ₹50,000), then open an Incognito Window and register Account B. Copy Account B's FSP-XXXXXXXX number.

    Step 2Test Transfers & ScamShield

    Send a normal ₹1,000 transfer, then try typing Urgent KYC refund in the Note field to trigger the ScamShield warning modal.

    Step 3Run Transaction Simulator Presets

    Open the Transaction Simulator tab inside this portal and test the Safe, Suspicious, High-Risk Fraud, and Fraud Ring presets.

    Step 4Inspect in the Bank Analyst SOC Portal

    Switch to the Analyst Portal to inspect live KPIs, the Fraud Network Graph, ATO & Device Intel, and the AI Investigator.
